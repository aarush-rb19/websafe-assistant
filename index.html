      Stay safer online

      Habits that protect accounts, devices, and personal data.

      Your Security Habit Progress 0%

      A strong password is unique, long, and never reused. Treat every account as if a leak on one site could open the rest.

      MFA adds a second check after your password so a stolen secret is often not enough to get in.

      Your phone and laptop hold sessions, files, and saved logins. Locking them down closes a wide path into your accounts.

      Default settings often share more than you intend. Review who can see your data and which apps can use it.

      Public posts are a map for scammers: job titles, travel, family, and photos can be used to impersonate or guess answers.

      Lock down recovery paths and watch for signs someone else is trying to take over your identity.
